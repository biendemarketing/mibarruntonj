# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS in one file (from the explicit request "crea un menú en HTML"); no framework, no build step, works by opening the file or serving it.

## Users

Diners of the Peruvian restaurant Mi Barrunto (Spanish- and English-speaking) browsing the menu on a phone, at the table or deciding whether to visit. Secondary: the owner sharing a link instead of the handwritten notebook.

## Product Purpose

Present the restaurant's complete menu (42 handwritten notebook pages, transcribed to transcripcion.txt) as a fast, readable, bilingual one-page web menu: dish, ingredients, price, and service notes. Success = a guest finds a section, understands a dish, and sees its price in seconds.

## Positioning

The real kitchen notebook, dignified: every dish, combo (rondas), bilingual quirk, and note the handwritten menu carries — nothing invented, nothing omitted — in a warm criollo picantería presentation, not a sanitized generic template.

## Operating Context

Read on a phone while choosing what to order. Section order follows the notebook: Sandwiches, Peruvian Breakfast, Entradas, Sopas, Comida Criolla, Parrillas, Ceviches, Mariscos, Rondas Criollas, Bares, Broaster, Sides, Bebidas.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single static page, web only (user: "solo web", no print stylesheet required).
- Bilingual ES/EN: Spanish dish names lead, English explanation follows.
- ~170 items across 13 sections; must stay scannable.
- Prices exactly as transcribed. Some prices were partially covered by highlighter in the photos; the menu uses the best-effort reading and they must be verified by the owner before publishing: bistec c/papas fritas y arroz, anticucho c/rachi, parrillas c/papa a la huancaína, Rondas Criollas page, Tripleta Marina, Ronda marina.
- Items crossed out in the notebook (Tallarín Verde con Pollo broaster, Alitas Picante, Porción Arroz chaufa, Crema Acevichada) are excluded.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Mi Barrunto.
- Voice: warm criollo, bilingual, keeps the notebook's own wording.
- User-pinned visual direction: "Criollo cálido" — Peruvian reds, paper/cream ground, typographic character of a recipe notebook (recetario), picantería atmosphere.

## Evidence on Hand

- 42 photos of the handwritten menu (working-folder .jpeg files).
- transcripcion.txt — faithful full transcription, uncertain prices flagged with (?).
- No dish photography and no logo files exist; do not fabricate photos, awards, or claims.

## Product Principles

1. The menu is the product: every dish, price, and note ships; nothing invented, nothing omitted.
2. Criollo warmth, not corporate polish: the kitchen's own voice stays legible.
3. Phone-first scanning beats decoration in content regions.
4. Translation is a service: English explains, Spanish names the dish.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

High contrast on paper tones; semantic section headings; readable at arm's length on a phone; works offline as one file.
