# Surface brief — menu.html (Carta Mi Barrunto)

## Scope and mode

Persuade. One static page carrying the whole carta: 13 notebook sections, ~170 real dishes, bilingual. Read at the table or while deciding whether to visit.

## Audience, job, action

Spanish/English diners, phone-first. Job: find a section, understand a dish, see its price in seconds. The action is the visit's own: scan the amber index spine, read a shelf, decide what to order. Content truth lives in transcripcion.txt (42 photographed notebook pages); no dish photography exists.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment

"Retablo de Mi Barrunto" — the roll's assigned direction (index 7), locked by the user over the model pick, the split-flap challenger, re-roll and canon. Memorable moment: painted wooden doors open in two staggered beats over the name set in red and gold leaf, while an amber index spine travels with the reader and announces the next shelf before it arrives.

## Constraints

- Bilingual ES/EN: Spanish dish names lead, English explains.
- Prices exactly as transcribed; six highlighter-damaged readings are listed in the final report for owner verification.
- Crossed-out notebook items excluded (Tallarín Verde c/Pollo broaster, Alitas Picante, Porción Arroz chaufa, Crema Acevichada).
- Bares combos: notebook prices illegible, so the two "elige 1 + 1" combos ship as combinations choosing, price on request.
- Raised lines kept (donors named in the round): margen index spine (mondo); day/night colorway (kit); amber next-section announcement (algorave); one corridor with per-shelf origin tickets (moonbazaar); two-phase door reveal, legible at rest (raku).
- Single self-contained file, no build step, web only.

## Unresolved decisions

- Six uncertain price readings wait on the owner (listed in the finish report).
- No photography and no logo exist: the surface is carried by painted CSS structure and typography alone.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The menu is an open ayacuchano retablo box: painted wood and gold leaf are the page's real structure, every section a shelf and every niche a small criollo altar — refusing the category's hero-plus-card-grid menu and its flat price list.

OWN-WORLD: Retablo red #a8321e field; painted-wood #6b4426 structural frame; gold-leaf #c89532 trim and rings; lime-plaster #f6ecd9 panels; andean green #2f4a3a accents; a candlelit night register of the same box. Components: plain niches with gold fillets, painted zigzag ribbon headers, two-beat swinging plank doors, price pills ringed in gold, per-shelf origin tickets naming the cuaderno pages, a painted sun medallion close. Type: Grandstander carved display, Alegreya Sans body with italic English.

STORY: The guest lands and understands this is one real, complete criollo carta; the amber spine puts any of 13 sections a thumb away; dish, English gloss and price read at a glance; the owner shares a link that feels like the notebook dignified rather than a template.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Plank doors swing open in two staggered beats above "Mi Barrunto" in red on gold-trimmed plaster; tricolor painted ribbon; the first shelf (Sandwiches) already starts with real dishes and price pills; the index spine stands at the left on desktop and as a sticky amber-marked chip strip on phones.

FORM: The roll's assigned direction (position 7 of the dealt list), locked by the user; seed:e0ba222399fc50da.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
